Privacy Policy for App Lock A2Z

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Mohammad Anas built App Lock A2Z as a Free/Utility application. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and how it is stored.

1. Information We Access

App Lock A2Z may access certain information and device features that are required to provide its core functionality, including:

  • Information about applications installed on your device
  • App usage information through Usage Access
  • Camera access for the Intruder Selfie feature
  • Photos, videos, and files selected for the Private Vault

We use this information only for the functionality described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Installed Applications and App Lock

App Lock A2Z accesses information about applications installed on your device so that you can view available applications and select the applications that you want to protect with App Lock.

The app may monitor the protected applications on your device to determine when a protected application is opened and to display the App Lock screen according to your selected settings.

This information is used to provide App Lock and app-monitoring functionality. We do not sell your installed-application information or use it for advertising or analytics purposes.

App Lock A2Z does not upload your installed-application information or app usage information to our own servers.

3. Usage Access

App Lock A2Z uses Android Usage Access to detect when a protected application is opened. This is necessary for the App Lock feature to work correctly.

Usage information is used for App Lock and app-monitoring functionality and is not uploaded to our own servers.

4. Camera Permission — Intruder Selfie

The camera permission is used by the Intruder Selfie feature. When the feature is enabled, App Lock A2Z uses the front camera to capture a photo after 3 incorrect PIN attempts.

Captured Intruder Selfie photos are stored locally on your device and can be viewed through the Intruder Logs feature.

Intruder Selfie photos are not uploaded to our own servers.

5. Private Vault

The Private Vault feature allows you to protect selected photos, videos, and files on your device.

Files handled by the Private Vault are stored locally on your device. App Lock A2Z does not upload your private vault files to our own servers.

6. Data Storage

Information and files required for App Lock A2Z features may be stored locally on your device.

We do not operate our own server for uploading or storing your Intruder Selfie photos, Private Vault files, installed-application information, or app usage information.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal or sensitive information. We do not share your locally stored Intruder Selfie photos or Private Vault files with third parties through our own servers.

The app may use third-party services, such as advertising services, which may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

8. Advertising

App Lock A2Z may display advertisements using third-party advertising services, including Google AdMob.

Third-party advertising providers may collect or process information according to their own policies. Their collection and use of information is governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored Intruder Selfie photos and Private Vault files remain on your device until you delete them through the app or otherwise remove the app data or uninstall the application.

You can manage or delete locally stored content using the relevant features provided by App Lock A2Z.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures within the app to protect locally stored data and to provide the security features offered by App Lock A2Z.

However, no method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Children's Privacy

App Lock A2Z is a utility application and is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our own servers.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
